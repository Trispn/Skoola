## Access Control Models for Educational Content

### Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
- **Public Access:** Open to all users without restrictions
- **Private Access:** Restricted to specific users or groups
- **Paid Access:** Monetized content requiring payment
- **Institutional Access:** Available to specific organizations
- **Time-Limited Access:** Temporary access with expiration dates

### Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC):
- User attributes (role, department, grade level)
- Resource attributes (content type, sensitivity level)
- Environmental attributes (time, location, device)
- Dynamic permission evaluation

## AI Features in Educational Technology Platforms

Based on research into leading AI EdTech tools, Skoola will integrate advanced AI capabilities to personalize learning, automate tasks, and enhance the overall educational experience for students, teachers, and administrators.

### Key AI Features to Integrate:

1.  **Adaptive Learning Paths:**
    *   AI-driven platforms assess students' skill levels in real-time and tailor instructional content to meet individual needs.
    *   Dynamically adjust curriculum, pace, and difficulty based on student performance and learning style.
    *   Provide personalized recommendations for resources and activities.

2.  **Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS):**
    *   Offer personalized, one-on-one tutoring experiences.
    *   Provide immediate feedback on assignments and quizzes.
    *   Identify misconceptions and offer targeted interventions.
    *   Simulate human tutors by understanding natural language queries and providing relevant explanations.

3.  **AI-Powered Assessment and Feedback:**
    *   Automate grading of various assessment types, including essays and open-ended questions, using natural language processing (NLP).
    *   Provide detailed, constructive feedback to students on their strengths and areas for improvement.
    *   Identify patterns in student errors to inform teaching strategies.
    *   Generate personalized practice questions and quizzes.

4.  **Content Curation and Recommendation:**
    *   Intelligently curate relevant educational content (articles, videos, exercises) based on student profiles, learning goals, and progress.
    *   Recommend courses, modules, and resources to students and teachers.
    *   Identify and suggest trending or high-quality content from the platform and external sources.

5.  **Personalized Learning Analytics:**
    *   Provide deep insights into student engagement, performance, and learning behaviors.
    *   Predict potential learning difficulties or drop-offs.
    *   Generate actionable reports for teachers and parents to support student progress.
    *   Visualize learning progress and identify areas requiring attention.

6.  **Automated Administrative Tasks:**
    *   Automate routine tasks for teachers, such as attendance tracking, scheduling, and basic communication.
    *   Assist administrators with student enrollment, record keeping, and resource allocation.
    *   Generate reports on overall platform usage and effectiveness.

7.  **AI-Driven Content Generation (for Teachers/Creators):**
    *   Assist teachers in generating lesson plans, quiz questions, and study materials.
    *   Suggest content improvements based on student engagement data.
    *   Automate the creation of diverse learning materials to cater to different learning styles.

8.  **Speech Recognition and Natural Language Processing (NLP):**
    *   Enable voice-activated commands and interactions within the platform.
    *   Support language learning through pronunciation feedback and conversational practice.
    *   Analyze student responses in spoken or written form for deeper understanding and assessment.

9.  **Virtual Assistants/Chatbots:**
    *   Provide 24/7 support to students for common queries, technical issues, and navigation assistance.
    *   Act as a learning companion, answering questions related to course content.
    *   Guide users through platform features and functionalities.

10. **Plagiarism Detection:**
    *   Utilize AI to detect plagiarism in student submissions, ensuring academic integrity.
    *   Provide detailed reports on originality and source matching.

These AI features will be integrated across various aspects of Skoola, from personalized student experiences to efficient teacher tools and robust administrative support, making the platform highly intelligent, adaptive, and user-centric.
