## **What are educational platforms?**

Educational platforms are all digital tools created to facilitate, enhance, and streamline learning, teaching, and training experiences. While many educational platforms cater primarily to students, schools, and universities, they also offer features for a wide range of users, including professionals looking to develop new skills and individuals exploring diverse topics.

In fact, many companies have made the switch to digital solutions when it comes to workplace training. Today, more and more businesses are recognizing, such as Learning Management Systems (LMS) and Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs), benefit and revolutionize how teams train.

In this list, we'll explore the best learning platforms for every purpose, featuring their prices and key features to help you make informed decisions.

[SC Training]() is a highly regarded, award-winning educational platform used by both large and small institutions globally. Accessible to all users, it gives easy-to-use design solutions and templates to make elearning content creation as easy and effective as possible.

Another cool thing about the platform is that it focuses on microlearning, so its courses are designed to be mobile-friendly, making it super easy for anyone to study anytime, anywhere. And guess what? This educational platform even has games and quizzes in between courses to make learning more effective and interesting for learners. How awesome is that?

On top of offering a [wide variety of courses](), SC Training also offers an intuitive [Creator Tool]() that you can use to create your own online learning courses. You can either create the courses manually or opt for the platform's AI-powered content generator tool. Say goodbye to complicated authoring methods and hello to a quick and easy process instead.

Also, if you don’t have the time to build your own courses from scratch, you can opt to take advantage of SC Training’s [Course Creation Services](). Through this add-on feature, their team of expert instructional designers can design and launch your courses for you along with additional onboarding and account support.

Your elearning platform has a considerable impact on the learning experience. If it’s clunky, your students will feel frustrated before they even start learning. But if it’s smooth and easy to use, learning becomes something they actually enjoy — and that’s the goal, right?

It’s not just about your students. A solid platform makes teaching easier, helping you focus on what you do best: **sharing knowledge and creating impact.**

That’s why choosing the right platform is so important. It’s not just about features or pricing — you need to make sure it matches the overall learning experience you want to deliver. 

To help you make the right choice, we’ve rounded up the top ten online learning platforms that make teaching online simpler, smoother, and way more effective.

## What value do online learning platforms bring?

In a paper titled [Online Learning Platforms: Reconstructing Modern Higher Education](), Zi-Yu Liu, Natalya Lomovtseva, and Elena Korobeynikova argue that online learning platforms can change the way higher education works in the future. They said the greatest benefits of this type of tool include “_greater freedom of access, lower education prices, the possibility of dividing the content of the e-course into modules, the flexibility of education, the ability to keep up with the modern pace of life, and the ability to define criteria for assessing knowledge.”_

## How to choose the best online learning platform

In general, you should look at the structure, course content, community, and price when evaluating which online learning platform is best suited for you. This will apply regardless if you’re using it to learn or to monetize your content! 

Choosing the right online learning platform will generally mean balancing these four aspects in a way that works best for you.

### 1\. Structure of the platform

[Course structure]() is important because it leads to a better [learning experience]() and more effective delivery of professional development programs. As an example, you can probably piece together knowledge on almost any topic through YouTube, but it would lack a structured and progressive environment. This would make it hard for students to learn independently. 

### 2\. Available course content

Looking at the course content is key to whatever you’re planning to learn or teach. All online degree programs and course materials are somewhat unique in how they approach any given subject. Take web development or programming, for example. There are so many principles, languages and frameworks that you can learn that it can’t simply be covered by individual courses. Some specialization is necessary.

If you’re planning to deliver a course through an online learning platform, it will help to look for ones that offer similar topics already. You can also look for platforms that offer similar setups. Free or mini-courses attract a much different audience than Bachelor’s degree programs! 

### 3\. Community support

Community makes the learning process much more fun and effective. When you’re stuck and can ask your peers for help, it reinforces what you’ve just learned with another perspective. Plus, doing something with a cohort of students is much more motivating than going alone.

From the teaching perspective, you can leverage [learning communities]() to empower brainstorming, independent problem-solving, and passion-building for the course topic.

### 4\. Pricing

Price is another key factor in selecting course materials that work for you. There are many free courses available, but paid ones tend to be more engaging and of higher quality. Some courses offer free trials while others offer refunds if you don’t like it. Some courses give you lifetime unlimited access while others rely on a subscription model or only work for a limited time.

Whether you’re monetizing or purchasing a course, you need to consider the value that the course provides to the student. Is the topic of interest, and does it provide enough content to be worth the pricing model offered?

When you’re selling through an online learning tool, you’ll also want to consider if there are any added costs. Sometimes the cost is accrued per sale, and sometimes it is a monthly fee. 

## What’s the difference between online learning platforms and learning management systems?

When you’re researching which online learning platform is right for you, it’s important to know how it differs from other similar solutions such as [learning management systems]() (LMS) and [online course websites]().

An online learning platform is a user-facing environment where students can learn new skills. In comparison, a learning management system is a back-end solution that provides all the necessary tools to the online learning platform so it can function smoothly. This would provide features like uploading videos, creating quizzes, grading assignments and much more.

Online learning platforms are similar to online course websites. In practice, the difference is that the online learning platforms tend to offer more flexibility than course websites. Online course websites are used for course creation, whereas online learning platforms can encompass all kinds of learning experiences, from short videos to online degrees.

## Educational Challenges in East and West Africa

Research indicates several significant challenges impacting education in East and West Africa:

### Common Challenges Across the Region:

*   **Poverty:** A major underlying factor, leading to issues like inability to afford school fees, lack of learning materials, and children being out of school to contribute to household income.
*   **Distance to School:** Long distances to schools, especially in rural areas, contribute to low attendance and high dropout rates.
*   **Poor Quality Environment:** This includes inadequate infrastructure, overcrowding, poor sanitation, and violence within school premises.
*   **Poor Quality Content:** Outdated curricula and inadequate learning materials hinder effective education.
*   **Poor Quality Processes:** A significant challenge is the lack of trained teachers, high teacher-pupil ratios, and poor school management.
*   **Educational Inequality:** Disparities based on gender, geography, and socioeconomic status are prevalent.
*   **Lack of Access to Educational Programming, Internet, and Materials:** These are critical barriers limiting the time children spend in education.
*   **Climate Change and Environmental Factors:** Droughts, floods, fires, heavy rain, and heatwaves impact education infrastructure and school attendance.

### Specific Challenges in West and Central Africa:

*   **Violence and Insecurity:** More than 14,000 schools have been forced to close due to violence and insecurity, impacting hundreds of thousands of children.
*   **High Number of Out-of-School Children:** West and Central Africa account for almost a quarter of out-of-school children worldwide, with nearly two million children being robbed of an education due to violence and insecurity.
*   **Teacher Training:** There is a growing need for teacher training in West and Central African countries.

### Specific Challenges in East Africa:

*   **Lowest Enrollment Rates:** Central and East Africa have the lowest education enrollment rates globally.

These challenges highlight the need for a comprehensive and context-aware educational platform like Skoola that addresses not only the technological aspects but also the socio-economic and environmental factors affecting education in these regions.




## Opportunities in African Education Technology

The African education technology (EdTech) landscape presents significant opportunities for innovation and impact:

*   **Addressing Resource Constraints and Teacher Shortages:** EdTech can help overcome limitations in physical resources and the scarcity of qualified teachers through scalable digital solutions.
*   **Expanding Access to Quality Education:** Online courses, mobile learning apps, and digital content are expanding educational access, especially in underserved communities.
*   **Bridging the Education Gap:** Adaptive learning platforms and low-cost digital tools are crucial in bridging the existing education gap.
*   **Increased Investment:** African EdTech startups are attracting record-breaking investments, indicating a growing recognition of their potential and scalability.
*   **Focus on Language Training and Teacher Incentives:** There's a growing emphasis from school systems and government ministries on language training and incentives for teachers, which EdTech can support.
*   **Democratizing Education:** Technology is democratizing education across Africa by making learning more accessible and affordable.
*   **Reshaping Learning:** Emerging digital tools promise to reshape how education is delivered and consumed.
*   **Teacher Empowerment:** Investing in teacher training and empowering teachers with technology is a sustainable way to build capacity.
*   **Digital Economy Preparedness:** Building an inclusive and forward-thinking education system through technology can prepare students for success in the digital economy.
